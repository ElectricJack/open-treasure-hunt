Blocks & Content

Blocks are sections of your treasure hunt. The Start Block is always visible. Other blocks can be locked and require solving puzzles to unlock.

Puzzles

Puzzles generate answers that can be used to unlock gates. Add puzzles to blocks, and their solutions will be collected by hunters.

Gates & Keys

Gates control access to locked blocks. Define which puzzle answers unlock each gate and how they combine to form the decryption key.

Before exporting, make sure:
